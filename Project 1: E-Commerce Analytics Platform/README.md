E-Commerce Analytics Platform using PySpark

Aim

To develop a scalable E-Commerce Analytics Platform using PySpark that ingests data from multiple sources, performs data cleaning and transformation, computes business KPIs, and generates analytical reports for business decision-making.


Objective

•	Ingest e-commerce datasets. 

•	Clean and transform data. 

•	Compute daily sales, customer, and product metrics.

•	Generate business insights. 

•	Build a modular analytics pipeline.
